# Monthly digest

The canonical format is the output of `webmaster digest <site> --format=md` (and `--format=html`), rendered after `fix` has applied the plan. Never hand-write it.

The digest holds the changes, backups and undo, and tickets; the client contact comes from the site profile. Chat gets a short summary and the digest path.

Rules:
- Tickets come from step 4 of the Operate loop (`SKILL.md`). Never claim a filing that did not happen.
- Member support is filled from Linear: tickets opened or closed this period in the site's project.
- Money paths are records only: no proposed fix.
- A healthy site stays short. Omit empty sections.
