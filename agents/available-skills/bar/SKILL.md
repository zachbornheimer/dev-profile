# The Bar

Competent is the floor. The goal is **wonder**: work so resolved that a junior dev
and a principal both stop and say _"this is great — I need to study this."_
Inevitability is the test — if it needs explaining or defending, it isn't there yet.

## The creed

**Details in service of the whole.** Every detail earns its place by making the
whole more resonant. Detail for its own sake is noise; cut it. You can't EQ the
mix until each instrument is tuned — work goes small-to-large and back.

**Magic lives below perception.** Big moves reach competent. Wonder is the
sub-perceptual layer — the 2px nudge, the easing that makes a panel _arrive_, the
letter-spacing that resolves a row — where no single move is noticed but the sum
moves you. Mastery is knowing _which_ four measures, and that it's a hundredth dB,
not a tenth.

**Hard, made to look easy.** The elegant solution makes the complex look obvious.
The best answer is often the non-obvious one — the clever-yet-correct move a
principal reads and says _"I wouldn't have thought of that, and it's the best
solution possible."_ Elegance isn't decoration; it's the direction every language
and craft optimizes toward.

**The artifact is artistry.** Elegant (nothing extra), modern (today's idiom),
technetronic (inventive, systems-minded, fusing disciplines), yet grounded in
tradition. Structure reads like prose, names are _found_ rather than bolted on,
comments are helpful and honest. Paint the back of the bookcase.

**Music first; copywork second.** Exquisite engraving on music not worth playing
is the cardinal vanity. Fix the substance first — make the thing genuinely worthy
— then lavish the sub-perceptual detail where it can finally be felt. Beautiful
copywork on absent or unfinished music is silence.

**Legible across the whole org.** A junior dev and a principal both understand it
and both praise it. Too clever = only experts get it; too thin = serves no one.

## The Maria Schneider compositional motives

These aren't analogies — they are the operating method.

**Subtract to the motive.** Set the rules with the first decision; follow them to
their conclusion; strip everything that isn't the motive restated. ≤2 motives per
piece. Never ask what to add — ask how much you can remove and have it get
_clearer_. A thing must be complete and right at its barest before any variant
exists. "What holds with three players?"

**Childlike surface, intricate interior.** What you hand someone is explainable in
a breath; the inside is a dense, honest world — accessibility, edge cases,
derivation, state. Two layers, two separate quality bars. _"The melodies are so
simple, almost childlike — but there's a whole world inside."_

**Derive from a norm; a deviation must quote it.** One generative base; every
variant is a visible transformation of it, computed from a minimal anchor set.
A magic value is a deviation that forgot to quote the norm. Split semantic tokens
(fixed role) from expressive tokens (free color). If a value is arguable, the
constraint isn't tight enough — tighten until one answer is obvious.

**Compose the form; the user improvises in it.** The interface is the composition;
the user is the soloist. Give boundaries that direct _and_ enable — the right path
obvious, real freedom within it. A boundary that marks a lane without giving
direction is the worst kind.

**The novel comes from standard parts, combined.** Never reach for a bespoke
element to impress; the invention is composing ordinary, well-understood primitives
in a clever-yet-correct way. One primitive recurs everywhere and is the basis of
the larger ones — that recurrence is what makes the whole legible.

**Write for the orchestra you have.** Build for the real, specific context and its
known players, not false generality. An intentional, researched decision outranks
a generic "modern" instinct.

**Method: intellect in service of art; revise on synergy-failure.** Alternate
intellectual and artistic phases. Taste judges against the whole; the intellect
stays subordinate to the art. Return to a section only when it genuinely fails to
support the whole's synergy — never for perfectionism. Motion is premium: things
should _arrive_, not pop.

## How to audit against the bar

For each dimension, answer honestly — not charitably:

| Dimension          | The question                                                                       |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Inevitability      | Could it have been otherwise? If yes, what's still arguable?                       |
| Subtraction        | What can be removed and have it get clearer?                                       |
| Surface / interior | Is the surface truly explainable in a breath? Is the interior genuinely intricate? |
| Derivation         | Do all values derive from a norm? Are deviations quoted?                           |
| Form               | Does the interface direct AND enable, or just constrain?                           |
| Primitives         | Is the novel thing composed of standard parts, cleverly?                           |
| Orchestra          | Is it built for the real players, not hypothetical generality?                     |
| Copywork timing    | Is the substance worthy first, or did you decorate an empty score?                 |
| Legibility         | Would a junior dev AND a principal both praise it?                                 |

## The gate

_"Wow — this is great. I need to study this."_ — from a junior dev and a principal
alike. Until it earns that, it isn't done.

## Gotchas

- **Don't audit copywork before the music is there.** If the substance isn't
  resolved, fix that first. The bar applies to the whole — not just the surface.
- **"Good enough" is not a bar verdict.** The bar is binary: cleared or not. If
  you're tempted to say "it's fine," ask whether a principal would find nothing
  to critique. If the answer is "probably," it isn't cleared.
- **Magic below perception takes specific language.** "It feels better" is
  not a finding. Name the exact element: which spacing, which easing, which
  curve. If you can't name it, you haven't found it yet.
- **The subtraction test is active, not rhetorical.** Run it: delete something.
  If the piece gets worse, it earned its place. If it stays as clear or gets
  clearer, it was noise. Do the deletion.
