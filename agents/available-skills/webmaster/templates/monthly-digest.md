# Monthly digest

You write the digest yourself after the fixes are applied: one page, plain language. Client tier: it goes to the client contact from the site profile. Other tiers: to Zach. Chat gets a short summary and the digest file path.

Sections: what changed (with backup location and undo command per change), tickets opened and closed, member support from the tracker, Records (money paths, no action), and needs-you.

Rules:
- Tickets come from step 4 of the Operate loop (`SKILL.md`). Never claim a filing that did not happen.
- Member support is filled from Linear: tickets opened or closed this period in the site's project.
- Money paths are records only: no proposed fix.
- A healthy site stays short. Omit empty sections.
