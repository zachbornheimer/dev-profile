`apps/php-cs-fixer` holds `.php-cs-fixer.dist.php`, the PHP-CS-Fixer config, tracked as `~/.config/php-cs-fixer`. It enables risky rules on top of `@PSR12` and `@PHP80Migration`, then applies WordPress-leaning overrides: double quotes, long `array()` syntax, one space around concatenation, aligned operators, and blank lines before `return`, `throw` and `try`. It also trims several phpdoc rules and drops empty phpdocs. The finder scans the config's own directory and excludes `vendor`.

`bin/dev-profile-php-fmt` is its consumer in the repo: when `php-cs-fixer` is installed and `~/.config/php-cs-fixer/.php-cs-fixer.dist.php` exists, it runs `php-cs-fixer fix --config=<that file>` (WordPress projects use PHPCBF only). `tests/php-fmt-basepath-fixture.sh` exists for that script; I did not read it, so I cannot say whether it exercises this config. The rules themselves have no test beyond PHP-CS-Fixer's own parse.
