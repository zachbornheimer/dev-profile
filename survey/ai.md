`apps/ai` is the AI-CLI environment loader, tracked as `~/.config/ai`. `env.sh` is sourced from shell startup: it uses `~/.cache/ai/keys.env` if it exists and is newer than the template, otherwise it calls `ai-keys::env` from `~/.config/zsh/ai-keys.zsh` to regenerate it, then sources the result. `keys.env.op` is the template. It holds only `op://` 1Password references (no secret values), covering OpenAI, xAI, Cerebras, Exa, PagerDuty and OpenRouter keys. `op inject` resolves them into the cache file, so the keys are exported for claude, codex, opencode, grok and their subagents. The template's comments also record that TypeSafe has no key of its own and goes through the OpenRouter key.

There are no tests for this directory. `config/mise.toml` syncs it with `track` and `encrypt = true`, and the repo-wide linters cover `env.sh` as shell. Failures show at runtime: if `ai-keys::env` fails, `ai-env::load` returns 1 and the shell starts without keys.
